01 / NYU CAPSTONE · 2026

Primrose

Contributed to a Genmab-sponsored proof of concept for finding EMA regulatory precedents. The team built an auditable manifest covering 225 products and 2,678 documents; a pilot across 12 products and 125 PDFs produced 6,505 citation-ready chunks. Hybrid retrieval combines section-aware chunking, BGE, BM25, and RRF. Formal retrieval and citation evaluation is still in progress.
