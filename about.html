HELLO, I'M YUANZOU

I believe better AI begins with better questions.

I'm pursuing an M.S. in Data Science at New York University, expected in May 2027. I build applied AI and data science projects with a focus on evidence: clear baselines, inspectable decisions, source provenance, and honest limits.

My current work includes Primrose, an NYU capstone on auditable retrieval for regulatory research, alongside AI-agent evaluation and machine learning experiments. Before graduate school, I earned a B.A. joint major in Mathematics and Data Science from NYU and studied at Mount Holyoke College.

Research and data internships taught me to turn messy inputs into useful decisions — and to make the reasoning visible along the way.
